# Miner Simulator — v53 update

This repository holds every script of the game (`src/`), the original place (`place/MinerSimulator_v51.rbxlx`)
and the ready-to-open updated place (`place/MinerSimulator_v53.rbxlx`). Nothing is built in Studio by hand:
the world, the models and the UI are generated by code. The only catalog assets used are the Night
Merchant's avatar (hat, hair, shirt, pants made by Roblox) and the level-up chime from Roblox's licensed
sound library; both have a built-in fallback.

## What changed in v53

- **Night wall**: the wall now runs along the whole front of the mine (corner to corner) and a slab lid
  covers the entire pit. Both exist only at night; by day the wall is underground and invisible.
  Sizes are in `GameConfig.NIGHT_WALL.Wings` and `GameConfig.NIGHT_WALL.Lid`.
- **Prices ×2.5**: pickaxes, backpacks, boosts, Repair Kit, Food and potions (rounded to 5 coins).
  Ore sell prices and rewards are unchanged.
- **Night Merchant**: a real Roblox avatar (fedora, hair, dark jacket) with idle and wave animations;
  a detailed built-from-parts character is shown until the avatar loads, or if it cannot load.
  Asset ids are in the `CONFIG` table of `MerchantNpcBuilder`.
- **Night Merchant menu**: five cards as in the reference — Repair Kit 215, Treasure Map 625, Food 115,
  Miner Potion 375, Shield Potion 400 — plus the 70% ore buyback bar. Book, Top Hat and Morning Bonus
  are no longer sold (hats and bonuses already owned keep working). Texts, colours and the treasure
  settings are in `MerchantConfig`.
  - *Shield Potion*: for 5 minutes mining adds no dirt to the backpack; a bubble shows on the character.
  - *Treasure Map*: hides a chest on the island for the buyer and tells the direction and distance;
    digging it up pays 1.3–2.4× the map price plus XP. One treasure per player at a time.
- **Level-up sound**: a soft chime (`ProgressionConfig.SOUNDS.LevelUpSoft`). To use your own sound again,
  put its id into `LevelUpCustom.Id`.
- **Energy** regenerates slower: 2.0/s in the mine (was 3.6), 5.0/s on the surface (was 9.0),
  +0.20/s per Energy skill level (was 0.35). See `GameConfig.ENERGY`.

## Fastest way to install

Open `place/MinerSimulator_v53.rbxlx` in Roblox Studio. Every script is already in its place.
Saves are compatible: the DataStore name and key are unchanged, the profile only gained one field (`Boosts`).

## How the pieces fit

- **Day/night**: `ReplicatedStorage` attributes `Phase`, `PhaseEndsAt`, `PhaseLength`; clients compute the
  remaining time with `workspace:GetServerTimeNow()`. At nightfall the server teleports every living player
  to the base, raises the wall, enables the blockers, and checks the mine zone every 0.5 s.
- **Sky**: the sun moves with the phase. The day phase runs from morning (07:30) to evening (17:30) on the
  sky clock; the night phase always runs under a night sky (21:00 → 04:00); sunrise comes when the mine opens.
  The hours are `ClockStart` / `ClockEnd` in `GameConfig.PHASE_LIGHTING`.
- **Wall**: the server switches collisions at once; each client plays the lift animation, dust, rumble and
  camera shake. Invisible 60-stud blockers stand behind the wall and around the whole pit, and the old
  force-field lid still covers the pit, so the mine cannot be entered from any side at night.
- **Shop**: the original pavilion and the original carousel menu (step on the pad or press the Shop button).
  The menu gained a third tab, "⚡ Бусты", which opens the boosts window.
- **Boosts**: the server stores the end time and publishes `Boost_<Id>_EndsAt` / `Boost_<Id>_Total` on the
  player. A repeat purchase extends the boost. The save stores remaining seconds, so a boost pauses while
  the player is offline. The Night Merchant potion is a boost too and gets its own chip.

## Decisions worth knowing

- **Pickaxes** keep their 10 levels; "epic" effects (glow pulse, heavier particles) apply to levels 8–10.
- **Backpack models** are the original v51 models, untouched.
- **Mining sounds**: the update adds no mining sounds. The per-rock tap sounds are the ones v51 already had.
- **Sparkles in the backpack window**: Roblox does not render particles or lights inside a ViewportFrame,
  so glowing ores get twinkling "✦" UI sparkles over the real 3D icon.
- **Russian text**: everything added or touched is in Russian. Older screens that this update does not
  touch (shop cards, skills, achievements, prestige, Night Merchant, sell kiosk) keep their existing text.
- **UI sounds** use built-in `rbxasset://sounds/...` files listed in `GameConfig.SFX`.

## Verification

Nothing here was run inside Roblox Studio — there is no Studio in the build environment. What was checked:

- `tools/check.sh` — luau-lsp type check with the Roblox API definitions: zero errors and zero warnings in
  all new scripts, and no new type errors in the edited ones.
- `lune run tools/smoke/smoke.luau` — executes the real builders outside Roblox (pickaxes and backpacks
  1–10, ore icons, wall and lid, plaza, merchant character, menu icons, treasure marker) with property
  and enum validation: all pass. The wall check asserts that by day every wall part is below ground and
  invisible, and that at night the wall reaches both corners of the pit.
- `lune run tools/smoke/verify_place.luau place/MinerSimulator_v53.rbxlx` — the built place loads and
  contains every script.
